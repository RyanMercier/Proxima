# CUTS: condensation log for the BLOCKCHAIN'26 camera-ready

Baseline: the mechanical llncs port of the submitted IEEEtran source compiled
to 17 pages; after the Phase B reviewer fixes and the Phase C fast-path
correction it reached 21 pages. The camera-ready must fit 10 pages including
figures and references, so roughly half of the rendered volume came out.
Every entry lists location (IEEE numbering where applicable), approximate
before-size, what was removed or condensed, and the rationale. No reviewed
number, table value, or plotted datum was changed anywhere; content removed
from the paper survives in the extended version (cited as the technical
report at the repository URL, reference [proxima-tr]).
